How stock-v2 works—and what it does not know

Stock setup scores only the ticker: trend structure, distance from key moving averages, RSI, 10/20-session price momentum, 20/60-session relative strength versus SPY, and recent completed-session volume trend. A single-day move contributes no more than 8 momentum points. Market permission is calculated separately, and entry posture combines the two without rewriting either score.

ATR-based buy zones, invalidation levels, reward/risk, and sizing are planning references—not support guarantees or order instructions. Earnings dates may be estimated. Fundamentals, valuation, sector-relative strength, taxes, slippage, and unscheduled news are not scored.
